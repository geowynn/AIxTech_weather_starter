# Weather Starter: 20 Visual Themes

1. **Forecast Slate**  
   Description: A restrained, modern dashboard look that feels dependable and data-first.  
   Design considerations: Color background uses cool slate, steel blue, and muted cyan accents; typography is a clean sans-serif with medium-weight headings; card styling is flat with thin borders, subtle blur, and low shadow; layout density is high with compact panels and tight spacing.

2. **Aurora Night**  
   Description: A cinematic night-sky theme with luminous weather highlights against deep darkness.  
   Design considerations: Color background uses midnight navy, violet, teal, and soft electric blue; typography is a crisp sans-serif with slightly widened headings; card styling is translucent glass with glowing edges; layout density is medium with a few spacious hero moments.

3. **Coastal Breeze**  
   Description: A light, breathable theme that feels fresh and easy to scan.  
   Design considerations: Color background uses seafoam, sky blue, sand, and white; typography is a friendly sans-serif with open counters; card styling is soft white with gentle radius and light shadows; layout density is medium-low with generous breathing room.

4. **Storm Radar**  
   Description: A serious operational style built around clarity and monitoring.  
   Design considerations: Color background uses charcoal, storm gray, amber warnings, and radar green; typography is condensed for labels and regular for body; card styling is hard-edged with separators and status chips; layout density is high for rapid scanning.

5. **Sunlit Minimal**  
   Description: A bright, minimalist theme that keeps attention on the forecast data.  
   Design considerations: Color background uses white, pale warm gray, soft blue accents, and muted gold; typography is neutral with strong hierarchy; card styling is almost shadowless with subtle dividers and very light borders; layout density is medium and clean.

6. **Mountain Lodge**  
   Description: A grounded, natural theme that feels outdoorsy and slightly rustic.  
   Design considerations: Color background uses pine green, bark brown, cloud white, and slate blue; typography is humanist with a warm tone; card styling is textured with soft borders and slightly thicker panels; layout density is medium with structured content blocks.

7. **Metro Transit**  
   Description: A dense city-dashboard aesthetic with efficient information presentation.  
   Design considerations: Color background uses off-black, concrete gray, signal blue, and accent red; typography is compact with strong numeric emphasis; card styling is square with crisp dividers and minimal rounding; layout density is very high for power users.

8. **Glass Horizon**  
   Description: A polished premium interface with layered translucency and soft depth.  
   Design considerations: Color background uses deep blue gradients, frosted white, cyan highlights, and subtle lavender; typography is elegant and light-weight; card styling uses glassmorphism, blur, soft borders, and floating surfaces; layout density is medium, airy, and composed.

9. **Retro Weather Map**  
   Description: A nostalgic theme inspired by vintage broadcast graphics and classic atlases.  
   Design considerations: Color background uses warm beige, muted teal, brick red, and ink black; typography is serif for headings and simple sans-serif for data; card styling is paper-like with thin outlines and restrained texture; layout density is medium with clear zones and classic symmetry.

10. **Arctic Signal**  
    Description: A cold, precise theme with a high-tech polar feel.  
    Design considerations: Color background uses ice blue, white, graphite, and glacier cyan; typography is sleek and geometric; card styling is sharp and luminous with frosted highlights; layout density is medium-high with strong structure and contrast.

11. **Golden Hour**  
    Description: A warm, optimistic theme that leans into sunrise and sunset tones.  
    Design considerations: Color background uses peach, amber, soft coral, and warm slate; typography is rounded and friendly; card styling is soft-shadowed with slightly rounded panels and warm surfaces; layout density is medium and inviting.

12. **Monsoon Ink**  
    Description: A moody, atmospheric theme with dramatic contrast and saturated weather cues.  
    Design considerations: Color background uses deep indigo, raincloud gray, cyan rain accents, and danger orange; typography is bold with strong weight contrast; card styling is dark layered with subtle gradients and defined borders; layout density is medium-high and intense.

13. **Citrus Static**  
    Description: A bright, energized theme that feels sunny and alert without becoming playful.  
    Design considerations: Color background uses lemon, tangerine, white, and a small amount of leaf green; typography is crisp sans-serif with compact headings; card styling is clean with sharper corners and light contrast; layout density is medium for quick scanning.

14. **Midnight Tides**  
    Description: A deep ocean theme that uses layered blue-black water tones for a quiet, premium feel.  
    Design considerations: Color background uses navy, deep teal, slate, and silver highlights; typography is refined sans-serif with quiet weights; card styling is smooth with subtle inner borders and soft glow; layout density is medium with calm spacing.

15. **Desert Heat**  
    Description: A warm arid theme that brings sun-baked earth tones into the weather interface.  
    Design considerations: Color background uses sand, terracotta, ochre, and dusty rose; typography is humanist sans-serif with sturdy numerals; card styling is matte with minimal ornament and lightly rounded corners; layout density is medium-low with generous openness.

16. **Winter Glass**  
    Description: A crisp cold-weather theme that feels frosty, bright, and spacious.  
    Design considerations: Color background uses pale ice blue, white, cool gray, and a sharp sapphire accent; typography is light sans-serif with high clarity; card styling is frosted and reflective with slim borders; layout density is medium and breathable.

17. **Electric Rain**  
    Description: A high-energy storm theme that pairs dark surfaces with vivid electric accents.  
    Design considerations: Color background uses near-black, cobalt, neon cyan, and violet; typography is bold sans-serif with strong contrast; card styling is glossy with high-contrast edges; layout density is medium-high and technical.

18. **Paper Almanac**  
    Description: A quiet editorial theme that feels like a printed weather journal translated into digital form.  
    Design considerations: Color background uses parchment, faded ink, muted blue-gray, and restrained sepia; typography is serif-led with readable body text; card styling is paper-like with delicate rules and soft shadow; layout density is medium with careful hierarchy.

19. **Sunset Harbor**  
    Description: A reflective coastal theme that uses dusk colors and soft gradients to relax the interface.  
    Design considerations: Color background uses salmon, dusk blue, deep plum, and warm cream; typography is rounded sans-serif with relaxed spacing; card styling is softly illuminated with gentle blur; layout density is medium and calm.

20. **Verdant Pulse**  
    Description: A fresh green-forward theme that feels alive, clean, and slightly botanical.  
    Design considerations: Color background uses emerald, moss, mint, and dark forest green; typography is modern sans-serif with balanced weights; card styling is crisp with subtle depth and light borders; layout density is medium-high with structured clarity.
